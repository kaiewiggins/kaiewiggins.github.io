The community storytelling board has been a huge success (winner so far: Arts on the Avenue in Ladysmith, where I had to erase the board three times to fit everyone's ideas in!) and it's been such a pleasure meeting readers, writers, creatives, community members, and travellers over the past couple of months. It's always fun to hear when you've seen a book in local (or international!) schools, met one of the authors at a school visit, or even met one of my authors friends at another market. I particularly loved seeing stock tick *down* at the local bookstores, as we got word out about local writers and y'all went out to shop local. Now more than ever, investing in our communities makes such a difference! Thank you!

I'll be slowing down a smidge on markets for the fall, but stay tuned for THE COOLEST (spookiest?) Halloween event at the Parksville Museum on the weekend of October 16-18!

My fall slate is filling up quickly (now that the back-to-school hustle is hitting!), so if you're looking for one-off event programming, or continuing workshops or mentorship, please do reach out as soon as you are able! You can find more information and inspiration on my [Bookings](/bookings.html) page; current and upcoming examples include festival speaking, creative workshops, and even some one-to-one mentorships with young writers. Speaking of, I've just sent out my reading list for a "Magic House Caretaker"-themed anthology workshop inspired by Diana Wynne Jones' *The House of Many Ways* for my Creative Writing for Children fall term and it's going to be so fun!
